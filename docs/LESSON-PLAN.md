# BrickL — plan for lessons 10–16

Lessons 1–9 are built. This file is the layout for the rest, so each one can be built the same way.
Every lesson has the same 3 steps: **Idea → Play → Build**, in English and German.

How help works by tier:
- **Tier 1 (lessons 1–5):** shadow outlines show where each part goes.
- **Tier 2 (lessons 6–11):** no shadows; hints show the area to look at.
- **Tier 3 (lessons 12–16):** no shadows, extra wrong parts in the tray, the goal is a whole machine.

Hints always come as a ladder: nudge → blink at the spot → show shadows. The hint is offered from the 2nd failed test.

## Tier 2 — Mechanisms

### Lesson 10 · Levers
- **Idea:** A lever is a bar that turns on a pivot. A long side is easy to push; a short side is strong.
- **Play (side view):** a beam on a pivot pin with a heavy box on one end. The child moves the pivot (drags it to another hole) and pushes the other end with a finger.
  - Goal: lift the box with a "small push." Too close to your hand = too heavy.
  - Task 2: make the box go up high (pivot near the box = small lift, near the hand = big lift).
- **New to build in code:** beam on a pivot, force = push × distance rule (same idea as the crank in lesson 6).
- **Build:** a 9-hole beam on a pin, a small toy on one end. Test: "Where is it easier to push: near the pin or at the end?"

### Lesson 11 · Push and pull (crank and rod)
- **Idea:** A turning wheel with a rod on it makes something slide back and forth. Steam trains and engines work like this.
- **Play (side view):** a gear with a pin, a rod from the pin to a slider in a track. Motor on the gear.
  - Goal: make the slider move far enough to hit the bell (pin farther from the centre = longer push).
- **New to build in code:** rod that follows a pin; slider limited to a track.
- **Build:** a 24 gear, a short beam as rod, a pin, and a beam as the track. Test: "Move the pin closer to the centre. Does the rod move more or less?"

## Tier 3 — Machines (combine your tools)

Each machine lists the tools it needs. It unlocks after those lessons are done.

### Lesson 12 · Crane
- **Needs:** Strong gears (2), Worm gear (7), Crank and winch (6).
- **Play (side view):** a tower is given. Build a winch that lifts a load to the top and holds it there; then a second motor raises the boom (up/down only, no turning left/right).
- **Goal checks:** load at the flag, motor off, nothing falls; boom at the marked angle.
- **Build:** a small crane on a 9-hole beam with the worm winch from lesson 7.

### Lesson 13 · Elevator
- **Needs:** Crank and winch (6), Strong gears (2), Up and down (8).
- **Play (side view):** a shaft with 3 floors. Build a lift that stops at floor 2 and floor 3.
  - Task 2: add a counterweight so a weaker motor can do it (new idea: weight on the other side helps).
- **New to build in code:** counterweight that reduces the force needed.
- **Build:** a string lift in a tower of beams, with a small bag of bricks as counterweight.

### Lesson 14 · Forklift
- **Needs:** Up and down (8), Strong gears (2), Turn a corner (9).
- **Play (side view):** the forks ride on a rack. The motor sits at the bottom and must reach the rack around a corner (bevel gears), with enough strength to lift a pallet.
- **Goal checks:** pallet lifted to the shelf; forks don't drop.
- **Build:** forks on a rack with a crank at the side; lift a box onto a book.

### Lesson 15 · Steering
- **Needs:** Up and down (8), Turn a corner (9).
- **Play (top view):** a car seen from above. A steering wheel turns a gear; the gear moves a rack left/right; the rack turns the front wheels.
  - Goal: drive the car around one corner on the road (tap Go, the car follows the wheels).
  - Task 2: steer the other way.
- **New to build in code:** top-view car, wheels that turn with the rack, simple driving path.
- **Build:** a small car front with a gear rack steering. Test: "Turn the steering wheel right. Which way do the wheels point?"

### Lesson 16 · Plane controls
- **Needs:** Levers (10), Push and pull (11), Turn a corner (9).
- **Play (side view, then top view):** a control stick connected with rods to the flaps.
  - Task 1 (side view): pull the stick back → the tail flaps go up → the plane's nose goes up.
  - Task 2 (top view): tilt the stick left → one wing flap up, the other down → the plane turns left.
- **New to build in code:** stick with two directions, rods to flaps, small animation of the plane reacting.
- **Build:** a stick on a pin with a rod to one flap (a short beam on a hinge or pin). Test: "Pull the stick back. Does the flap go up or down?"
